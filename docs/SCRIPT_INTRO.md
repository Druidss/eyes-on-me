# MISSION BRIEFING: EYES ON ME

---

### **WHO YOU ARE**
You are **Agent Miller**, an undercover operative. You have successfully infiltrated the Velorian Ministry.

### **THE SITUATION**
You are in the office of **Director Vane** for a routine security review. He is cold, highly suspicious, and watching you closely.

### **YOUR OBJECTIVE**
Gather as much intelligence as possible for your post-briefing evaluation:
* **Listen:** Memorize names, dates, and other clues Vane mentions.
* **Observe:** Scan the room for visual clues.

---

### **HOW TO SURVIVE: SUSPICION**
* **Avoid Detection:** Scanning the room while Vane makes direct eye contact increases his **Suspicion**. If suspicion peaks, you will be arrested.
* **Safe Windows:** Look around **only when Vane is looking away or is distracted**.
* **Return Eye Contact:** Immediately look back at Vane as soon as he turns to face you.

*Stay calm. Do not get caught.*
